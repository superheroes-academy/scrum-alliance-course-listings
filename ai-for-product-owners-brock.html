Frequently asked questions

What is included?

Four hours of live instruction and practice, course materials, the Scrum Alliance microcredential upon successful completion, and a two-year Scrum Alliance membership or membership extension.

Do I need AI experience?

No. You should be comfortable using a web browser and willing to experiment. We will work with prompting and judgment practices that transfer across tools.

Do I need a CSPO certification?

No. Product experience will help you connect the examples to practice, but Scrum Alliance lists no formal prerequisite.

Which AI tool should I bring?

Bring access to a generative AI assistant such as ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude. A free account is sufficient for the learning activities.

Can my organization register a private group?

Yes. Contact info@superheroes.academy for private delivery, group pricing, invoices, or accessibility needs.

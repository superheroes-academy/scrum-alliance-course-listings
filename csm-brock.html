Your mastery map

What You Will Learn and Practice

Eight connected capabilities take you from understanding the framework to using it with a real team.

01

Scrum foundations and empiricism

Define Scrum, connect it to the Agile Manifesto, and experience transparency, inspection, and adaptation.

02

Accountabilities and collaboration

Explore how the Product Owner, Developers, and Scrum Master work together to create valuable Increments.

03

Events, artifacts, and commitments

Understand the purpose and boundaries of Scrum's events, artifacts, Product Goal, Sprint Goal, and Definition of Done.

04

A complete Scrum simulation

Perform Sprint Planning, Sprint Review, and Sprint Retrospective across three Sprints while building a product with your team.

05

Quality and technical debt

See the impact of an unclear Definition of Done, then improve quality and reduce surprises in later Sprints.

06

Facilitation and group decisions

Practice techniques that help teams form, make decisions, surface different perspectives, and move forward together.

07

Product Backlog work

Explore Product Backlog refinement, stakeholder collaboration, Product Owner authority, and the role of the Sprint Goal.

08

Impediments and true leadership

Analyze organizational impediments and distinguish when a Scrum Master facilitates, teaches, mentors, or coaches.

Learn by doing

How the Learning Works

Every concept moves through a deliberate learning loop, so Scrum becomes something you can reason about and use—not just terminology to remember for a test.

Step 01

Experience

Enter a game, simulation, or team challenge before being handed the answer.

Step 02

Reflect

Notice what happened, compare perspectives, and make the invisible dynamics visible.

Step 03

Explain

Connect the experience to Scrum theory, purpose, boundaries, and practical choices.

Step 04

Apply

Try again with new insight, adapt your approach, and carry the learning forward.

CSM Exam

After completing the approved course, you will receive access to the online Scrum Alliance CSM exam. The exam has 50 multiple-choice questions, a 60-minute time limit, and a passing score of 74%. Scrum Alliance includes two attempts when taken within 90 days of the course.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are the CSM exam and certification fees included?

Yes. Your approved live course, CSM exam fee, practice test, and two-year Scrum Alliance membership are included. Scrum Alliance includes two exam attempts when they are taken within 90 days of the course.

How big are the classes?

Classes typically include 6–15 participants so there is room for personal attention, discussion, team practice, and questions about applying Scrum in your context.

What support is available after the course?

You receive course resources, personalized one-to-one tutoring support until you pass the exam, and lifetime access to the Superheroes Academy Slack community of Agile professionals.

I am a project manager or do not work in software. Is this relevant to me?

Absolutely. Scrum is used well beyond software, and this course welcomes people from project management, operations, finance, education, healthcare, product, and many other fields. The activities help you reason about Scrum in your own context.

What is the course format?

This is a two-day, live-online course delivered through Zoom and Mural. Expect breakout teams, games, simulations, short teaching segments, structured reflection, and frequent debriefs—not a slide marathon.

Do I need prior Scrum experience?

No. The course begins with shared foundations and is designed for new Scrum Masters, Scrum Team members, leaders, and experienced practitioners who want to strengthen their understanding.

What is the refund policy?

We offer a 100% money-back guarantee on your CSM purchase any time before you receive your certification. If your package includes a microcredential and access has already been granted by Scrum Alliance, the refund is reduced by $75 for each granted microcredential.

Can I reschedule or ask about a discount or payment plan?

Yes. We understand that plans change, and we offer several ways to make training more accessible. Contact info@superheroes.academy to discuss rescheduling, group or self-pay discounts, and no-interest payment arrangements.

What AI options are available?

The AI Plus and AI Accelerator Pro plans extend the class with Max Good AI Coach access, AI learning resources, personal support, and—on the Pro plan—an eligible Scrum Alliance microcredential. See the learning-plan comparison for current inclusions.

When will new course dates be posted?

New dates are added regularly. If none of the available dates works for you, email info@superheroes.academy and our team will do its best to help.

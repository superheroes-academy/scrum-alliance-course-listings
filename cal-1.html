Agility starts with leadership

Move from knowing agile to leading differently.

Agile transformation rarely stalls because people have not heard the right vocabulary. It stalls when leaders keep rewarding certainty, control, local optimization, and risk avoidance while asking teams to adapt.

CAL 1 helps you examine that tension honestly. You will explore how your own choices shape culture, how to lead through complexity, and how to make change more participatory, resilient, and humane.

This is not a leadership lecture dressed in agile language. It is a facilitated space to compare perspectives, work with real situations, and translate principles into choices you can make immediately.

You will leave with a clearer leadership stance, practical ways to grow teams and culture, and the Scrum Alliance CAL 1 credential.

The leadership shift

Three tensions every agile leader must learn to hold.

Hover or focus each tension to see the shift.

01

Direction and discovery

How do you lead when the path isn’t fully knowable?

The shift

Create meaningful direction without pretending the path is fully knowable. Learn to invite evidence, learning, and adaptation into how work is led.

02

Accountability and autonomy

Where do you hold the line—and where do you let go?

The shift

Set clear boundaries and outcomes while giving capable people room to decide, experiment, and own how value is created.

03

Performance and humanity

Can high standards and psychological safety coexist?

The shift

Build environments where high standards, psychological safety, candid feedback, and sustainable performance reinforce one another.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need prior agile experience?

No. CAL 1 has no prerequisites. The course is useful for experienced agile leaders and people taking their first serious step into agile leadership.

Is this an official Scrum Alliance credential?

Yes. Complete the full approved course and participation requirements to earn the Scrum Alliance Certified Agile Leader® 1 credential.

Is there an exam?

No final certification exam is required for CAL 1. Your credential is awarded after successful completion of the approved live course.

How is the course delivered?

The course runs live online over two days, from 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM Alberta Time (ABT, UTC−6). Expect active participation, dialogue, small-group work, reflection, and application to real leadership situations.

Who will teach the course?

Erkan Kadir is the Scrum Alliance-authorized CAL educator, and he co-delivers the learning experience with Brock Argue. Both are Scrum Alliance Certified Scrum Trainers, Certified Enterprise Coaches, Certified Team Coaches, and ICF Professional Certified Coaches.

What is included?

Your registration includes 16 live course hours, course materials, the CAL 1 credential, a two-year Scrum Alliance membership, and ongoing access to the Superheroes Academy community.

Can my organization arrange a private cohort?

Yes. Contact info@superheroes.academy to discuss a private course tailored to your leadership context.

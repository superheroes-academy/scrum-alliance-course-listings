Course summary

Turn good intentions into skillful intervention

Agile coaches rarely struggle because they lack advice. The harder challenge is recognizing what a person, team, or situation needs—and responding without taking away ownership.

In this practical four-hour course, you will explore six complementary coaching stances—Advising, Leading, Teaching, Mentoring, Facilitating, and Coaching—and practise moving between them intentionally. You will work with realistic scenarios, test language that builds clarity and trust, and leave with a personal action plan for applying the skills in your own context.

Brock's promise: You will not spend four hours talking about coaching. You will coach, facilitate, advise, teach, mentor, lead, reflect, and try again—with useful feedback along the way.

Meet Your Trainer

Brock Argue is a Scrum Alliance Certified Scrum Trainer, Certified Enterprise Coach, and Certified Team Coach, as well as an ICF Professional Certified Coach and ORSC-trained systems coach. He brings more than two decades of experience in software development, leadership, facilitation, training, and organizational coaching.

Over the past two decades, Brock has supported agile journeys at organizations including the Federal Reserve Bank of New York, WestJet Airlines, ADP, Suncor Energy, Benevity, and the Calgary Public Library. As a co-founder of both Superheroes Academy and FUSE Facilitation, he brings a grounded, human-centered approach to coaching, mentoring, and professional learning.

His style is warm, direct, and deeply practical. Brock cares about the work behind the buzzwords: helping people notice what is happening, tell the truth skillfully, learn from experience, and make one better choice at a time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there an exam or an additional credential fee?

No. There is no exam, and the Scrum Alliance microcredential fee is included in your course price.

Do I need an existing Scrum Alliance certification?

No certification is required. Familiarity with Agile or Scrum is helpful, and the course is accessible to Scrum Masters, coaches, leaders, facilitators, and change agents from many backgrounds.

What is the course format?

This is a four-hour, live-online course delivered through Zoom and Mural. Expect visual collaboration, focused teaching, breakout practice, coaching conversations, realistic scenarios, structured reflection, and feedback—not a slide marathon.

How big are the classes?

Classes are intentionally small so there is room for personal attention, meaningful practice, feedback, and questions about applying the skills in your context.

What materials are included?

You receive a digital participant guide, the Agile Coaching Growth Wheel and Superheroes Support Model, facilitative listening tools, practical prompts, an action-planning template, and access to the Superheroes Academy Slack community.

When will I receive my microcredential?

After you successfully complete the live course, Superheroes Academy will submit your attendance to Scrum Alliance. Scrum Alliance will then add the microcredential to your profile.

What support is available after the course?

You retain access to your course resources and the Superheroes Academy Slack community, where you can continue learning with Agile professionals and trainers.

What is the refund policy?

We offer a 100% money-back guarantee before your Scrum Alliance credential is granted. Once the credential has been issued, the course is considered completed and is no longer refundable.

Can I reschedule or ask about a group discount?

Yes. Contact info@superheroes.academy to discuss rescheduling, team enrollment, group pricing, or accessibility needs.

Can my employer receive a tax invoice?

Yes. Contact info@superheroes.academy if your organization needs an invoice or additional purchasing documentation.
